Subject: Recording a tenant's own insurance policy at API move-in — best approach?

Hi team,

When a tenant brings their own insurance policy at online move-in, we'd like to
record the carrier and policy number against the rental. Two questions.


1. CAN THIS BE DONE AS PART OF process_move_in?

When a tenant picks one of the facility's plans, we send its insurance_id and
everything is recorded.

When they say they have their own policy, we first tried sending
insurance_id: null. That's rejected:

    (4) An insurance option must be selected for move in
        (insurance_id: An insurance option must be selected for move in)

Sending insurance_id: "-999" instead works and the move-in succeeds — though
we'd like to confirm that's the value you'd expect us to use. Either way, we
can't find anywhere in the request to put the tenant's carrier name and policy
number, so those never reach you. Is there a field for them that we've missed?

We'd prefer this over a second call after the lease exists.


2. IF NOT — CAN WE USE PUT ledgers/:ledger_id/private_insurance?

That looks like the right endpoint for this, but it returns a 403 for us:

    PUT /v1/25e2ece8-668b-467d-934b-81dd0024edbc/ledgers/0250e2dd-0fe8-4ef1-923c-ce2cf051c8a1/private_insurance

    → 403  "You are not authorized to access this page."
       request_id: 5f7ca268-6700-9f3c-bd68-33296bc378ad

It isn't a general permissions problem: the same credential reads that ledger
fine (GET ledgers/{id}, /insurance_enrollment_history,
/insurance_services_and_discounts all return 200) and writes move-ins fine.

So: is this endpoint available to us, and what permission enables it? A full
example request — headers and complete body — would be ideal.

One thing we're unsure of: insurance_term is required, but the tenant only
gives us their carrier and policy number at checkout, never an expiration date.
What do you recommend there?


WHY WE'RE CHASING IT

With -999 and no proof on file, the ledger shows current_insurance: null and an
empty enrolment history, while the tenant carries
exclude_from_insurance_auto_enroll: false. That looks like a tenant who told us
they're already covered could be auto-enrolled into a paid plan.

Lease 6670 on facility 25e2ece8-668b-467d-934b-81dd0024edbc is a live example.

Thanks,
Chrys
